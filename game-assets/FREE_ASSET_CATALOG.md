# Free World Maps, Audio & VFX Asset Catalog

This catalog replaces generated-art dependency with openly licensed external assets and source manifests. Assets are selected for commercial-friendly use; each asset must still be checked against its source license before redistribution.

## Realistic / modern world-building

- **Poly Haven** — CC0 3D models, HDRIs and high-resolution PBR textures. Use for realistic terrain, rocks, roads, buildings, vegetation materials and lighting.
- **Poly Haven Rocky Terrain** — CC0 terrain material with diffuse, displacement, normal and roughness maps.
- **Poly Haven Rocks Ground 01/04/05** — CC0 rocky-ground materials for realistic terrain layers.
- **OpenGameArt 180+ Seamless Hex Tiles** — CC0 realistic terrain tiles covering desert, volcanic, forest, lava, grass, dirt, water and rocky environments.
- **OpenGameArt Open World Tileset** — CC0 HD-style top-down open-world terrain, paths, water, elevation and buildings.

## Nostalgic / retro world maps

- **Kenney Map Pack (180 assets)** — CC0 overworld/terrain/road tiles and spritesheets.
- **Kenney Cartography Pack** — CC0 fantasy cartography tiles, icons, vectors and paper textures.
- **Kenney Minimap Pack** — CC0 minimap tiles in multiple styles.
- **Puny World Tileset** — CC0 16x16 old-school RPG overworld tiles with grass, trees, elevation, rivers, sea, buildings and resources.
- **Isometric Pixel Terrain** — CC0 isometric terrain with animated water and farm variations.
- **Overworld Map** — CC0 retro overworld sample covering desert, grass, dirt, cliffs, water and lakes.

## Sound effects

- **Kenney 50 RPG Sound Effects** — CC0, including footsteps, metal, cloth, books, coins and interaction sounds.
- **OwlishMedia Sound Effects Pack** — CC0 ambience, footsteps, impacts, water, UI, sci-fi and technology sounds.
- **LEGIT Audio The Shop Free SFX** — CC0 shop ambience, drones, appliances, engines and room tones.
- **OpenGameArt Free Sounds collection** — collection of CC0/free-use game sound packs; retain source-specific attribution data where applicable.

## VFX

- **70 Animated 2D Game Effects** — CC0 particle/animation effects.
- **Lots of Game Effects** — CC0 aura, blast, fire and explosion spritesheets.
- **PVFX Foundry** — CC0 pixel VFX including combat, elemental, defensive, support, portal, beam, water and environmental effects.
- **itch.io CC0 Effects catalog** — CC0 effects filtered for sprites, sound effects, textures, tilesets and backgrounds.

## Map library targets for BizX

The runtime asset manifest should expose at least these map families: alpine, desert, Mediterranean, forest, tropical, coast, island, river valley, mountain, volcanic, snowy, grassland, farmland, industrial city, modern city, harbor, ancient/ruins, fantasy, space colony, and retro RPG overworld.

Each family should have day/night, weather and nostalgic variants where the source pack permits it. Avoid redistributing copyrighted maps; use licensed tiles/materials as building blocks and generate game-specific layouts procedurally.

## Licensing policy

Prefer CC0/public-domain assets for bundled redistribution. For CC-BY or other attribution licenses, store the required credit in the repository's asset credits file and do not silently relicense the original asset. Do not copy assets with unclear licenses.
